# Extra Space Storage: not a Paradox career site

*Checked on 12 November 2026, from `https://careers.extraspace.com/search-jobs` and one job page, in a browser.*

- **The site does not run on Paradox.** It loads files from AppVault (`workspace.appvault.com`, `appvault-chatbot.vercel.app`); no file, link or text mentions Paradox or Olivia.
- **The DNS points to Cloudflare.** `careers.extraspace.com` is an alias of `careers.extraspace.com.cdn.cloudflare.net`, with no link to `sites.paradox.ai`.
- **The jobs and applications are on Workday.** The search shows **548 results**, with Workday ids (`R-82994`), and the apply link leads to `extraspace.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/ESS_External/…`.
- **Paradox's case study says otherwise.** It lists Conversational ATS, Traitify Assessments, **Career Site Studio** and Conversational Scheduling for Extra Space: the case study may be out of date, or Paradox may only be used for a step that the site does not show.
- **For scraping:** this site is not a target for a Paradox scraper; its jobs would be collected through Workday.
